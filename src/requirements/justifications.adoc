ASCIIREQ-JUSTIFICATIONS-0800:: _Justification as free text_
+
--
It is RECOMMENDED to set <<ASCIIREQ-BODY>> justifications
as free text,
out of the <<ASCIIREQ-ITEM>>.
--
+
* *Version:* 0.1
* *Actors:*
** <<ASCIIREQ-WRITER>>

This pattern clearly separates the _what_ and the _why_.
The _why_ is important to track.
But it is important to keep the _what_ separately,
so that it can be clearly identified and processed
without being polluted by _why_ concerns.

Apart from <<ASCIIREQ-BODY>> justifications,
dedicated justifications
may also be attached with <<ASCIIREQ-ATTRIBUTE>>s
(see <<ASCIIREQ-TRACEABILITY-1000>> for instance).
